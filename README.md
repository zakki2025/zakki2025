## Market Insight \& Consumer Behaviour Analysis for CP Group's Poultry Products

Read the intro in 妈妈厨房里的"鸡"智革命:正大品牌鸡肉调理类深加工制品市场及消费研究以北京市宝妈为例-PPT.pdf

• Designed a mixed-method study based on the Theory of Planned Behaviour and 4P Marketing Mix; executed a three-stage stratified sampling across Beijing’s 16 districts; collected and cleaned 718 valid responses, ensuring data representativeness for the ”Mom” demographic with children aged 0–14.

• Constructed a path analysis model to evaluate complex causal relationships among latent variables (e.g., Price Sensitivity, Brand Trust, Purchase Intention); achieved excellent model fit (RMSEA=0.041, CFI=0.953, χ2/df = 2.21); identified that Promotional Activities and Price have stronger direct effects on purchase intention than long-term brand assets, revealing high economic sensitivity in the target segment.

• Applied Hierarchical Clustering validated by Elbow Method and Silhouette Analysis to segment consumers into 4 distinct personas (e.g., Efficient Career Elites, Budget-Conscious Housewives); synthesised findings into a go-to-market strategy proposing tailored product bundles and channel optimisations (e.g., community group buying for price-sensitive clusters) to maximise market penetration.
